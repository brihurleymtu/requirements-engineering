1. Stakeholders
Possible college equipment booking stalkeholders include:
-Students
-Lecturers
-Equipment Technicians
-Department Administrators
-Equipment Managers

2. Stakeholder needs and concerns
   
Student:
What might this person need?
To know which equipment is available to book.
What might this person be concerned about?
Being unable to get the equipment they need when it is required.

Lecturer:
What might this person need?
To be able to book the equipment they require for teaching.
What might this person be concerned about?
Not being able to book the equipment they require for teaching.

Equipment Technician:
What might this person need?
To know when or what equipment is due out and back.
What might this person be concerned about?
Any late or missing equipment.

Equipment Manager:
What might this person need?
To be able to maintain accountability for equipment.
What might this person be concerned about?
Any equipment being lost of used without authorisation.
