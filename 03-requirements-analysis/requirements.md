## 3. Requirements Surgery 
FIND -> DIAGNOSE -> ASK -> REPAIR -> CHECK
Worked example: "The system shall manage bookings efficiently."
FIND: What is the requirement trying to tell us?
Answer: The requirement is trying to tell us that currently bookings are not being managed efficiently.
DIAGNOSE: What is wrong with it?
Answer: We do not know what the client means by manage booking efficiently, who is involved in the booking
process? What is their role? What should they see on the system?
ASK: What clarification question could we ask?
Answer: Who will be using the system and what are their needs?
REPAIR: Once we understand the need, we can improve the requirement
CHECK: Ask, how could we verify this requirement?
-Who is involved?
-What must the system do?
-Does a condition apply?
-Why is this needed?
-How could we verify it?
